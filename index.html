🎨 Exhibits

AI Art Installations

Explore a curated collection of works at the intersection of machine learning, generative systems, and human expression.

🌌

Latent Cosmos

by Aria Chen & Neural Studio

A real-time diffusion model renders an ever-evolving cosmos shaped by visitors' voices and movements — no two moments are ever the same.

Generative Interactive Diffusion
🌿

Deep Roots

by Marcus Rivera

Cultural heritage meets transformer models. Indigenous patterns from 10 communities are remixed by an AI trained to honor — not erase — origin.

Culture NLP Heritage
❤️

Mirror Affect

by Yin & Yang Collective

A camera reads your micro-expressions and generates an abstract painting that represents your current emotional state in real time.

Computer Vision Emotion AI Painting
🌊

Synthetic Seas

by Priya Nair

Ocean data — temperature, salinity, and currents — is translated into ever-shifting soundscapes and visual poetry by a custom multimodal model.

Data Art Multimodal Sound
📜

Lost Languages

by Dr. Emeka Osei & LinguaLab

An LLM trained on extinct language corpora generates poetry and song in reconstructed tongues — a haunting dialogue between past and future.

Language LLM Poetry
🤖

Who Made This?

by the Open Ethics Collective

Can you tell the difference? 100 artworks — half human, half AI — are displayed without labels. Visitors vote, discuss, and discover the truth.

Ethics Interactive Philosophy
